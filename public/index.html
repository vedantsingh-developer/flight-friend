Terms of Use · Prototype draft

By creating an account, you agree to these draft terms. Age and Terms/Privacy/Safety confirmations are collected in sign-up. Creating an account also requires separate age, privacy, and safety confirmations. These terms do not remove consumer or other legal rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

1. What Flight Friend is

Flight Friend is a prototype for connecting people requesting an item with travelers already going between Indian cities. This build has persistent accounts and an opt-in traveler directory, but no live orders, bookings, payment processing, identity verification, customer-support desk, or delivery service. A listing or self-entered flight number is not proof that a person, ticket, flight, train, or offer is genuine.

2. Who can use it

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. Provide truthful information and use the service lawfully. Do not impersonate anyone, falsify travel details, mislead another user, or use another person’s identity without permission.

3. Travel and handoffs

Users are responsible for checking their own journey and agreeing on any lawful handoff. Never ask someone to carry an unknown, sealed, restricted, dangerous, illegal, or prohibited item. Do not conceal an item from airline, railway, airport, station, customs, or security staff or ask another person to bypass screening. Follow the carrier’s current baggage rules and applicable food-safety, transport, and local laws. A traveler may refuse or stop a handoff at any time.

4. Items, food, payments, and disputes

Describe requested goods accurately and check they may lawfully be carried. Perishable food is subject to safe packaging, timing, and carrier rules. This prototype does not take payments, hold funds, set binding delivery fees, issue refunds, insure goods, or resolve delivery disputes. Do not pay or share financial credentials through this site. A real service must publish payment, cancellation, refund, loss/damage, complaint, and escalation terms before launch.

5. Safe community conduct

No threats, harassment, discrimination, fraud, unlawful goods, or requests to break travel rules. Meet only in a public place you consider safe. Keep Aadhaar, ticket barcodes, booking references, home addresses, and bank details private. Use carrier safety staff or local authorities for urgent risks.

6. Profiles and availability

Directory visibility is optional. If you opt in, your chosen name and city-to-city route are visible to all website visitors until you unlist or delete your account. Your optional date and self-entered flight number are private to the developer dashboard and are not verified. A new profile’s activity metrics show “New”; a community score is not a credit score, background check, endorsement, or guarantee.

7. Accounts and moderation

Accounts require a password and truthful contact details. The operator may suspend a profile that appears to violate these terms, with any action subject to applicable law and user rights. The developer dashboard can suspend, unlist, or delete accounts and delete feedback.

8. Legal framework and important limits

Use is subject to applicable Indian law, including consumer-protection and personal-data rules as they apply. Nothing here waives statutory rights or limits liability where law does not allow it. The Constitution of India, including Article 21 as interpreted by Indian courts, is a legal reference—not a claim that this prototype has completed a constitutional or privacy-law assessment. For applicable offences from 1 July 2024, the general criminal-code reference is the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS); the Indian Penal Code (IPC) was replaced for offences governed by the new code from that date. Prior events and special statutes may differ. These references are not exhaustive and are not legal advice.

9. Operator details and complaints

The prototype owner has not supplied a legal entity name, registered address, support email, or grievance contact. Add and verify those details, response timelines, and a service jurisdiction before public launch. Do not rely on this prototype for real transactions.

Privacy Notice · Flight Friend prototype

This website uses Firebase Authentication for verified email and phone sign-in when configured. Firebase processes the email, password, phone number, verification email, and SMS code. Flight Friend stores the profile, consent selections, optional trip, and feedback in its encrypted app database.

Information stored

Firebase handles the password and verification factors; Flight Friend does not receive the password in Firebase mode. The app stores your name, verified email and phone, and age/Terms/Privacy/Safety confirmations. If you choose to list a trip, it also stores your airport route and optional travel date and self-entered flight number. Profile, trip, and feedback payloads are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage in the SQLite database. Email and phone lookup values are keyed HMACs. Account status, route visibility, and timestamps are stored as service metadata.

Raw Aadhaar numbers, ticket files/barcodes, payment credentials, and biometrics are not requested or stored. No UIDAI, airline, or railway identity/itinerary check is connected. Do not submit those details.

Who can see it

Your name and city-to-city route become public on the website only if you explicitly opt into the traveler directory and the account remains active. Optional date and flight number, account contact details, and consent details are visible only to the developer dashboard. Anonymous feedback contains its topic and message and is not linked to your account. Age, Terms, Privacy, and Safety confirmations are collected with account sign-up.

Storage and retention

Account and feedback data stay in the server SQLite database until the developer deletes the record or database. The dashboard can suspend/unlist/delete accounts and delete feedback. Anonymous visitor-consent receipts are stored separately; the dashboard can clear that receipt log. The encryption keys are held in the hosting service private environment settings. If those keys are lost, encrypted records cannot be recovered. Keep the database and keys private.

Sessions and external links

The app API uses HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookies; Firebase keeps sign-in state in the browser. The site does not use advertising analytics. External culture and legal reference links go to third-party sites whose privacy practices apply after you leave Flight Friend.

Your choices

You may browse without an account. Traveler-directory publication is a separate, optional opt-in. You may log out, ask the operator to delete your account, or unlist your route through the developer dashboard. The developer dashboard is protected by a password, but the developer dashboard has one administrator and no multi-factor authentication.

Before public launch

A production operator must publish a complete notice with the operator identity, purposes, recipients/processors, retention, safeguards, support/grievance contact, rights and withdrawal process, and any international transfers. The DPDP Act and Rules have phased commencement provisions; a qualified reviewer should confirm the provisions in force on the actual launch date. Do not collect raw Aadhaar numbers; connect only an authorized identity-verification design after specialist review.

Community Safety · Prototype draft

  • Only list real journeys you are taking; never invent a ticket, route, or identity.
  • Never carry an item unless you know exactly what it is and it is allowed by the carrier and law.
  • Do not carry weapons, contraband, dangerous goods, or anything a carrier prohibits; do not conceal items or bypass checks.
  • Protect ticket QR codes, booking references, Aadhaar, phone number, and home address. Share only what is needed, privately.
  • Choose a public handoff point, tell someone you trust, and leave if a situation feels unsafe.
  • Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, scam, or pressure another member.
  • Self-entered travel details are not verified in this prototype.
  • For immediate danger, contact the relevant carrier/security staff or local emergency services.
